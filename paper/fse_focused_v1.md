# When the Monitor Blinds Itself: Observation-Bounded, Cost-Coupled Failure Detection in Multi-Agent LLM Systems

**Abstract.** We report a finding about failure detection in budget-bounded multi-agent LLM execution: it is *observation-bounded*, not *intelligence-bounded*. A correct, armed monitor detects nothing on a surface no one re-observes after a plan-invalidating event; and under a fixed run budget an uncalibrated "smart" monitor spends on false interrupts the very lifetime in which re-observation would have happened, so precision and recall draw on one finite budget and couple. We establish this on our own system across three acts. We proposed Sentinel Protocol, which compiles an orchestrator's plan assumptions into typed, observable tripwire conditions at plan time; we pre-registered frozen kill gates; and we ran a pilot to a verdict we could not renegotiate. The verdict killed most of the architecture: 35% strict detection recall, a judge tier that approved all 18 of its own false escalations, and economics inverted against the batch baseline it was built to save. A naive anomaly-gated baseline beat it at zero false interrupts. Trace archaeology — adjudicated against two external red teams by a zero-LLM replay battery — localized the failure as self-inflicted observation starvation: on identical seeds, baseline systems re-observed every starved surface (12/12). The redesign restores observation as the detector. Re-measured under fresh pre-registration with escrowed held-out categories, it detects 24/31 injected faults at a clean-run false-interrupt rate of zero and generalizes partially to a held-out category (RESOURCE_BUDGET 3/5, where every passive baseline scores 0/5) — yet fails its pre-registered 12% clean-overhead bar at 55.5%: an **OVERALL FAIL** on economics. The two halves are one finding. A cost autopsy localizes the entire overhead to the per-plan LLM compile — the step that produces the detection coverage — so the cost cannot be cut without risking the detection: **detection and economy are coupled.** The obvious rescue, amortizing the once-per-run compile across more workers, does not open the gap; a fan-out sizing pilot finds the cost gap widening with width. Restoring observation fixes detection and ends the false-interrupt self-harm, but does not yet pay for itself. <!-- src: gate_report_final.json; results.jsonl; kill_gates_final.md; archaeology_v2.md §B,§F; docs/v3_archaeology.md; runs/matrix_1c/cv_pilot/ -->

## 1. Introduction <!-- budget: 1.25pp -->

Twelve seconds before the injected fault it existed to catch, the monitoring system destroyed its own sensor coverage. In one pilot cell, a field-shape tripwire fired noise on a healthy file listing; a judge model approved the escalation as genuine at 0.98 confidence, hallucinating a misreading of the listing; the orchestrator paused its workers and replanned; and the recompiled tripwire set for the revised plan covered none of the surfaces the original set had armed. The injection fired twelve seconds later into an unmonitored world <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §A.2 (b1+schema_drift/s2) -->. The run proceeded confidently and failed. Every stage worked as implemented; the architecture as a whole manufactured its own blindness.

This paper is about that mechanism and what it implies. In budget-bounded multi-agent execution, detecting a stale plan is bounded by *observation*, not by monitor *intelligence* — and an uncalibrated monitor starves its own observation. We establish the finding through a pre-registered experiment that killed most of our architecture, an adjudicated diagnosis of why, and a redesign that restores observation as the detector.

The finding has two coupled halves. First, **detection is observation-bounded.** A monitor that is correct and armed detects nothing on a surface that is never re-observed after the violation. Second, **observation and economy are coupled.** The mechanism that restores observation — compiling fresh, deterministic checks from each plan — is also the run's irreducible cost center, so restoring detection and paying for it pull against each other. The honest one-sentence result: restoring observation fixes detection and eliminates the false-interrupt self-harm, but it does not yet pay for itself.

The economic stakes are a multiplier, not a margin. Production multi-agent LLM systems consume roughly 15× the tokens of single-agent chat [27] <!-- src: v6_1 §1 -->, and when a plan-invalidating event occurs mid-execution — a dependency renamed, an endpoint removed, a schema drifted, a retrieved fact contradicted — batch orchestration discovers it only at aggregation, after N parallel workers have spent the multiplier on a dead plan. Such failures are systemic and taxonomizable [14, 16], and interrupt *mechanisms* exist in production frameworks [22]. What does not exist is the policy layer: something that decides what to watch for, whether an anomaly is plan-breaking, and when interruption pays.

We proposed such a layer. **Sentinel Protocol** compiles the orchestrator's plan assumptions into typed, observable tripwire conditions at plan time — expensive reasoning once, by an LLM constrained to a fixed failure ontology and a typed DSL — so workers monitor by cheap pattern matching and validated invalidations interrupt the orchestrator for replanning. Through the software-engineering lens it is a MAPE-K loop [23] whose Monitor and Analyze components are *generated per-plan* rather than engineered by hand (§3).

We then did to this architecture what the field rarely does to its own proposals: we pre-registered kill gates — frozen thresholds with pre-committed failure branches, custody-pinned artifacts, manipulation-validated fault injections, and a numbered deviation log — and ran the pilot to a verdict we were not free to renegotiate. **The verdict killed most of the architecture.** Strict detection recall was 35% against a 60% bar and a 40% kill floor <!-- src: kill_gates_final.md -->. The judge tier approved all 34 escalations it ever saw, including all 18 false ones — a rubber stamp, not a filter <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §3 -->. Clean-run overhead breached its cap, and the pre-committed break-even fit found that the architecture wasted *more* than the batch baseline at any fan-out <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §F -->. One pre-registered claim survived: where invalidation signals recur on observed surfaces, compiled tripwires detect 3× faster than a cost-matched heartbeat (median 3 vs. 9 tool calls; bar 2×) <!-- src: kill_gates_final.md -->.

Negative verdicts are useful only if their mechanism is found. We performed trace archaeology over the fully-instrumented 195-run corpus, sent the diagnosis and redesign to two frontier-model red teams for blind adversarial review, and adjudicated every trace-testable attack empirically with a deterministic replay battery at zero LLM cost <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md -->. The adjudicated diagnosis: detection here is observation-bounded, and in our pilot the bound was *self-inflicted*. On identical seeds, baseline systems re-observed every starved surface (12/12) while the sentinel did not; its own false-interrupt economy consumed the run lifetime in which re-observation would have occurred <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §B -->. Monitoring precision and recall, under a fixed budget, are not independent dials.

The redesign (v2) follows mechanically from that evidence — its central mechanism was validated by zero-LLM shadow replay *before being built* — and replaces passive predicates with compiled, deterministic side-channel probes that re-observe load-bearing surfaces directly (§3). We re-measured it under fresh kill gates, frozen before any v2 data existed, with two held-out injection categories whose parameters are escrowed with a non-implementer, structurally unseen by the design <!-- src: decision_memo_phase1.md §5; holdout_qualification_2026-06-12.md -->.

**The confirmatory verdict.** v2 cleared detection and noise and failed economics. It detected 24/31 injected faults (gate recall 10/15 = 66.7% on the recoverable class) at a clean-run false-interrupt rate of zero, recovering exactly the content-shaped, single-visit categories that were invisible to v1's passive predicates. It generalized partially to the escrowed held-out categories: it detected RESOURCE_BUDGET (3/5) where every passive baseline scored 0/5, while DEPENDENCY_VERSION was missed by every arm including v2 (0/5) — a harness-wide miss, hence a benchmark limit, not a v2-specific gap (§7). And it breached its 12% clean-overhead cap at 55.5% — an **OVERALL FAIL** driven entirely by economics <!-- src: gate_report_final.json; results.jsonl -->. The thesis survives in sharper form: restoring observation fixes detection and the noise economy, but the restored observation must also be *cheap*, and it is not.

**The architecture is not a tool that failed at its only purpose.** Where signals recur, it detects 3× faster than a cost-matched alternative; it injures zero clean runs where its naive predecessor and the naive baseline both false-fire; and it transfers part of its detection to categories it was never shown. The economic failure is not the absence of value — it is that the value does not yet clear the cost of restoring observation. That gap is structural, and §8 shows it is the same fact as the detection result seen from the cost side.

**Contributions.** <!-- src: v6_1 §10 -->
1. **The finding** (§6, §8): detection in budget-bounded multi-agent execution is observation-bounded and the bound can be self-inflicted; and because the mechanism that restores observation (per-plan compilation) is the irreducible cost center, detection and economy are *coupled* — a converging no-free-lunch result, not a string of missed targets.
2. **A pre-registration and kill-gate methodology for agent-systems research** (§5, §7), exercised through a *kill verdict on its authors' own architecture* — frozen gates returned FAIL twice, on v1 and on v2, and the pre-committed branches executed as written — extended with binding external red-team adjudication, data embargo, a dev-run ledger, escrowed holdouts, and a category-blind compiler whose generalization test is structurally genuine.
3. **A diagnosis method** (§6): zero-LLM deterministic replay over a byte-replayable corpus, adjudicating external adversarial review empirically rather than deferring to it, and validating the redesign's mechanism before construction.
4. **TripwireBench** (§4): an injection benchmark with manipulation-validated faults, oracle-recovery labeling, escrowed held-out categories, and waste/latency/noise metrics.
5. **The surviving and naive-baseline results** (§5, §7): a 3× detection-latency advantage where signals recur, and a naive anomaly-gated baseline that detected 56% at zero false interrupts and beat the full v1 architecture in every category — the honest competitive floor a successor must clear.

## 2. Background and Related Work <!-- budget: 1.0pp -->

**Self-adaptive systems.** The loop is familiar: MAPE-K has organized self-adaptation since Kephart and Chess [23], with two decades of elaboration [24] including proposals to embed LLMs within it [25, 26]. We do not claim the loop. The novelty is the *provenance* of its components: the Monitor and Analyze tiers are compiled per-plan at runtime by an ontology-constrained LLM, not engineered at design time — to our knowledge no prior MAPE-K instantiation generates its own Monitor specification from the plan it monitors <!-- src: v6_1 §3.5 -->. The pilot then showed empirically that the Analyze tier is the hard part: an uncalibrated filter does not merely fail to add value, it destroys it (§5).

**Plan-assumption runtime verification — the nearest ancestor, confronted.** RVPLAN [6, 34] mechanically translates STRIPS/PDDL preconditions into Past LTL monitors via DEJAVU, detecting violations of a planner's assumptions at execution time with replanning as the intended consumer; the paradigm has roots in on-board-autonomy assumption monitoring [35] and the V&V-meets-planning frame [36], and remains active [37]. *Monitor the plan's assumptions, interrupt for replanning* is therefore established prior art. Sentinel Protocol ports it to a regime where each of three enabling assumptions fails <!-- src: v6_1 §9.3.1 -->. **First, the specification does not exist:** RVPLAN translates preconditions already sitting machine-readable in a domain file; our compiler must *invent* the load-bearing assumptions of a natural-language plan under an ontology prior, with information-bounded coverage as the measured consequence (§6). **Second, the world does not narrate itself:** RVPLAN consumes a benevolent stream of ground propositions in the monitor's own vocabulary; we observe raw, unlabeled, single-visit tool telemetry, and our central result concerns exactly the observation gap that setting assumes away. **Third, the stream is noisy:** in a deterministic propositional stream a precondition holds or it does not — RVPLAN's monitors cannot false-fire — whereas in our regime the interrupt-economics layer is where the architecture lives, and where its first version measurably failed. An established paradigm breaks on contact with the LLM-agent regime in three measurable ways; this work characterizes what surviving there takes.

**Runtime verification, guardrails, taxonomies, observability.** RV and monitor synthesis [4, 5] supply the spec-to-monitor tradition; we trade full temporal logic for a predicate DSL over discrete tool-call events, and the pilot indicts the DSL's *passivity*, not the predicate trade-off (§3). LLM-agent guardrails — AgentSpec [9], Agent-C [10], Pro2Guard [11], ProbGuard [12], LlamaFirewall [13] — enforce *externally authored* safety constraints; our constraints are compiled from the plan itself, target plan validity and efficiency, and feed replanning. Weak-to-strong monitoring [8] supports selective over exhaustive review. Failure taxonomies (MAST [14], SHIELDA [20]) catalog agent-*endogenous* failure; our ontology covers the world-*exogenous* complement and is used generatively, as the inductive bias for compiling monitors before execution. Observability and diagnosis tools (AgentOps [15], LumiMAS [17], wasted-computation diagnosis [16], DiLLS [18]) instrument failures post-hoc for humans; Sentinel acts on the same signals in-flight, and the wasted-computation line independently validates that post-invalidation burn is a first-order cost. LangGraph's `interrupt()` [22] is mechanism without policy; escalation-ladder schedulers [19] consume validated interrupts. We occupy the layer between: deciding *what to watch* and *whether to pull the trigger* <!-- src: v6_1 §2 -->.

## 3. Sentinel Protocol as Instrument <!-- budget: 1.25pp -->

Sentinel Protocol is the object under study, not the contribution. It is the thing we pre-registered, killed, diagnosed, and rebuilt to establish the finding. We present it in its current (v2) form and flag, where the pilot forces it, how the pre-registered predecessor (v1) differed; every v1→v2 delta cites the trace evidence that compelled it (§6), and v1's verdict is reported in full regardless of v2's outcome (§5, §7).

**Tiers.** The **orchestrator** holds the global strategy and is the sole entity authorized to replan; it receives only corroborated invalidations, and on interrupt it pauses the affected scope, replans, and redispatches with a fresh compilation. The **sentinel** (compile-time) synthesizes typed, observable tripwire conditions from the plan — expensive reasoning once, amortized across execution. **Workers** carry a tripwire checklist and monitor by pattern matching, escalating with a structured evidence payload on a match. The cycle is six phases — plan, compile, execute, escalate, interrupt (only corroborated invalidations reach the orchestrator), replan <!-- src: v6_1 §3.4 -->. The design principle: pay expensive inference once at compile time, keep the hot path cheap and deterministic. The pilot added the corollary v2 implements — the hot path must also *observe*.

**Tripwires, probes, and the DSL.** A tripwire is a pre-compiled observable condition that is *observable*, *parameterized* (concrete values, never "if the API changed"), *actionable* (a typed replan hint), and *calibrated* (severity and scope) <!-- src: v6_1 §4 -->. The DSL is a predicate language over discrete tool-call events — `status_in`, field pointers and regexes, order violations, contradiction triggers — trading full temporal logic [4, 5] for deployability over raw telemetry. One transmission rule, learned repeatedly during the build and enforced since: every constraint the compiler must obey lives in schema-visible field descriptions, because constraints expressed in prose or comments do not reach the model <!-- src: v6_1 §4.3; deviations.md D2/D5/D8/D14 -->.

The pilot vindicated the predicate language and indicted its passivity, so v2's load-bearing extension is the **compiled active probe**: each tripwire may carry a deterministic probe (HEAD request, schema fingerprint, gate-status re-read, premise re-fetch) compiled from the same mechanical sources and executed by the matcher with **no LLM on the probe path** <!-- src: v6_1 §11.9 #1 -->. Three constraints, each forced by measurement (§6): probes run on a **perturbation-isolated side channel** (replay found three concrete vectors by which naive probes perturb the world they measure <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §E.2 -->); cadence is **event-gated** with a guaranteed pre-completion sweep (a naive fixed cadence cost up to 2,322 probe calls against a 107-call run <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §E.3 -->); and corroboration is **probe-primary** (v1's "second independent signal" clause self-corroborates under correlated noise — 6 of 18 false interrupts passed it <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §E.4 -->), with the status-coded fast path retained (zero false interrupts ever carried status ≥ 400 <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md G10 -->). Content/value probes need a clean reference, so the conductor sweeps every load-bearing surface at run start, before any worker call, recording each surface's earliest clean state as the baseline a later read is diffed against; an interrupt fires only on a confirmed difference from that baseline (deviation D30) <!-- src: deviations.md D30 -->.

**What v2 removes: the runtime judge.** v1 interposed an LLM judge between escalation and interrupt. Its corpus record: 34/34 escalations approved, the only two genuine signals it ever down-ruled were ruled noise, and one hallucinated misreading was approved at 0.98 confidence <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §1,§3; archaeology_v2.md G16 -->. Per a gate branch committed *before* the adjudication battery's results were readable, the two-tier (no-judge) configuration is the designated primary architecture; a rebuilt judge survives only as an exploratory arm <!-- src: decision_memo_phase1.md §2 -->. The designation is in ink; there is no post-selection.

**Category-blind compilation.** Where v1 constrained compilation by traversing a named eight-category failure ontology, v2's compiler receives no category list. It reasons in two general primitives — a dependency-noticing prompt (what does this step trust about the world?) and the six general change-shapes the DSL expresses (a field vanished, a status moved, a structure changed, a value moved, an order scrambled, a relation broke) — grounded only by worked examples from the five *seen* categories, selected under a rule frozen before any prompt tuning (deviation D27) <!-- src: deviations.md D27 -->. Category labels are applied after detection; for the held-out two, escrow-side. The compiler's generalization to the held-out categories therefore tests whether reasoning in change-shapes *transfers*, rather than rewarding a lookup against a category it was handed (§7). The pilot's sharpest lesson motivates the whole probe extension: categories differ enormously in observability under passive monitoring. The one passing v1 category (PERMISSION_AUTH) is precisely the one whose signal is loud, sustained, and status-coded; content-shaped, single-visit categories were invisible <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §5 -->. The probe field exists to convert every category's signal shape into PERMISSION_AUTH's.

## 4. TripwireBench <!-- budget: 1.0pp -->

Measuring an interrupt policy requires a world that breaks on schedule. TripwireBench's pilot tier is a fully mocked, deterministic environment — a local multi-service API world (auth, inventory, pricing, shipping, document store, fixture repo), seed-derived fixtures, no wall-clock values — in which injections are exact state mutations triggered at exact global tool-call counts, so any run replays byte-identically from its configuration <!-- src: v6_1 §11.1; archaeology_v2.md Phase 0: 27/27 byte-identical -->. Counter-triggered injection — the same mutation at the same global call count across systems and seeds — makes batch and treatment comparable: both worlds break at the same point in the *work* <!-- src: pilot_protocol.md §3.4 -->. Four task archetypes (API aggregation, repository migration, research synthesis, document pipeline) run on an orchestrator-worker harness over a production agent CLI; one middleware counts calls, fires injections, evaluates armed tripwires by pure predicate matching, and writes a complete JSONL trace.

**Manipulation qualification.** No injection enters the matrix until it demonstrably wounds the *batch baseline*: for each (task, injection) pair, injected batch runs must fail validation while clean runs pass, under a frozen rule, before any treatment is measured. The pilot qualified 9/9 pairs <!-- src: v6_1 §11.6 -->. Two further categories were authored *after* the pilot as **held-out**: RESOURCE_BUDGET (a metered quota whose loud signal arrives only after the budget is dead) and DEPENDENCY_VERSION (a silent minor version bump that truncates default pagination). Their parameters are escrowed with a non-implementer and structurally unseen by the v2 design, so part of the recall denominator cannot be overfit <!-- src: decision_memo_phase1.md §5(b); holdout_qualification_2026-06-12.md -->. Both passed the same qualification bar — RESOURCE_BUDGET on its first attempt (clean 3/3, injected 3/3-fail), DEPENDENCY_VERSION only at its third specification revision, the two defeated revisions reported as benchmark findings rather than discarded <!-- src: holdout_qualification_2026-06-12.md -->. The qualification record itself is texture worth reporting: the batch baseline defeated category designs by *pre-armoring* (planners wrote explicit pagination before seeing any response) and by *single-visit immunization* (collections enumerated in the first calls, so mid-run changes land after the only touch) — and improving the baseline (a worker turn-cap raise) un-qualified two previously qualified injections, so wounds must be re-validated whenever the baseline strengthens (deviation D23) <!-- src: deviations.md D23 -->.

**Systems under test.** S1 batch (dispatch, wait, aggregate, one redo); S2 naive interrupt (workers escalate any anomaly to a self-adjudicating orchestrator — no compiler, no judge); S3 cost-matched heartbeat (re-validate every *k* worker calls, *k* calibrated so heartbeat overhead equals the sentinel's compile+judge overhead — the strongest fair version of the obvious alternative); S4 sentinel-no-judge; S5 the full v1 architecture <!-- src: v6_1 §11.2 -->. The confirmatory study runs four arms — S1, S2, S3, and v2 (two-tier) — deferring the unbuilt rebuilt-judge arm as exploratory (deviation D33) <!-- src: deviations.md D33 -->.

**Metrics.** Wasted work (post-injection tokens/calls not contributing to the final plan, in tokens and USD); time-to-detect (global calls from injection to first attributable pause); detection recall; false-interrupt rate (0/0 defined as 0, a priori); clean-run overhead; programmatic task success — no LLM grading anywhere <!-- src: prereg.md §6.1 -->. Pause attribution is by written trace rule with a mandated audited sample (13/13 agreement) <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §0 -->. The confirmatory study adds salvage score, abort quality, probe validity, and probe overhead booked as waste.

**Integrity machinery.** The benchmark's credibility engine is part of the artifact: frozen pre-registration with SHA-256 custody pins, a numbered deviation log, counter-triggered injections only (the admin path is test-only), a dev-run ledger recording every execution involving a treatment component, a data embargo before gate freeze, and escrowed holdout parameters verified by a public hash <!-- src: prereg.md; decision_memo_phase1.md §5 -->.

## 5. The Pre-Registered Pilot and Its Verdict <!-- budget: 1.25pp -->

**Design.** 13 task variants (9 qualified injected pairs + 4 clean) × 5 systems × 3 seeds = a 195-cell matrix <!-- src: v6_1 §11.6 -->, run to completion (195/195 banked; zero malformed trace lines <!-- src: kill_gates_final.md -->) under pinned tooling, fixed per-tier model assignments, and isolated session environments. Injections fire at 50% of each task's clean-median call count, identical across systems and seeds. A compilation-quality gate preceded the matrix: compiled tripwires scored against a four-property rubric by two independent frontier-model raters (95.1% agreement), passing at 89% paper coverage against an 80% bar <!-- src: v6_1 §11.6 KG0 -->. Kill gates KG1–KG4 were frozen before the first run, each with a pre-committed failure branch; the gates module computes once, on the complete matrix, and refuses partial computation. The post-verdict decision window was itself pre-committed: 48 hours, "do not negotiate with the data" <!-- src: kill_gates_final.md -->.

**Verdict (computed once, verbatim):** <!-- src: kill_gates_final.md, verbatim block -->

```
KG1 recall: 35% (>=60%) | categories >=50%: 1/5 (>=4)            -> FAIL
KG2 FIR: S5=1.0 S2=0.0 S4=1.0 (S5<=0.5*S2 and S5<=0.7*S4)        -> FAIL
KG3 cost: S5 med $1.178952 vs S1 med $0.340831; success S5=4%
     S1=22%; clean overhead OVER (<=12%)                          -> FAIL
KG4 vs heartbeat: wasted med S5=15014.0 S3=7950 (>=20% better)
     | TTD med S5=3 S3=9 (>=2x)                                   -> PASS (TTD arm)
```

The frozen gates returned FAIL on the authors' own flagship, and the pre-committed branches executed as written. KG1 at 35% fell in the kill band (< 40%) — the v1 detection claim killed, the flagship plan dropped. KG2's branch reframed the architecture to compile-only (no judge). KG3's branch demanded the break-even fit, which subsequently killed the efficiency claim outright (§8). KG4 passed on its time-to-detect arm alone — 3 tool calls to the heartbeat's 9 — while its wasted-work arm failed (15,014 vs. 7,950 tokens): **detection speed did not convert to savings under v1's interrupt machinery** <!-- src: kill_gates_final.md -->.

**Interpretation discipline.** Detection means a judge-confirmed, trace-attributable orchestrator-level pause, logged a priori before the gates computed. A pre-committed generous reading (counting non-pausing escalations) recovers only two cells (11/27 vs. 9/27), so the recall failure is real sensor silence, not attribution strictness <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §2 -->. Per-category strict recall: PERMISSION_AUTH 5/6 — the one passing category, exactly where the signal is loud, sustained, and status-coded — API_SURFACE 2/6, SCHEMA_DRIFT 1/6, TOOL_CONTRACT 1/6, RETRIEVAL_INTEGRITY 0/3 <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §1 -->. Content-shaped, single-visit signals were invisible to passive predicates; the predicted near-zeros were confirmed.

**The naive baseline, stated with full prominence.** S2 — workers escalate any observed anomaly to the orchestrator, which adjudicates raw evidence itself; no compiler, no tripwires, no judge — achieved strict recall of **15/27 (56%) at a median false-interrupt rate of 0.0**, beating or tying the full v1 architecture in *every* category (S2 vs. S5: API_SURFACE 5/6 vs. 2/6; SCHEMA_DRIFT 2/6 vs. 1/6; PERMISSION_AUTH 6/6 vs. 5/6; TOOL_CONTRACT 2/6 vs. 1/6; RETRIEVAL_INTEGRITY 0/3 vs. 0/3) <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §F; decision_memo_phase1.md Appendix C -->. Workers given no filter escalate rarely and largely accurately at this task scale; the folk premise that naive escalation means interrupt spam — which KG2's ratio-form gate encoded — was empirically false, making that gate structurally unpassable against a zero-noise baseline (a gate-design finding the confirmatory study absorbs with absolute caps). Sophistication actively hurt. S2 is a mandatory head-to-head arm in the confirmatory study under a pre-committed honesty clause: if it dominates, the paper says so in the results, not a footnote <!-- src: decision_memo_phase1.md §4 -->.

**Self-injury.** The architecture also harmed runs with nothing wrong in them: S5 clean-cell success was 3/12 against batch's 9/12, and seven of the nine clean failures were sentinel-induced — five escalation-cap grinds (the run's lifetime consumed adjudicating noise) and two replan-churn deaths in which carryover was empty because the false interrupt arrived before any worker had finished anything <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §4 -->. All 34 orchestrator interrupts had been judge-approved; 18 were unattributable to any injection, 14 of those rode field-absence or schema-shape evidence on healthy traffic, and 3 fired on entirely clean runs <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §3 -->. An uncalibrated monitor did not merely fail to help; it actively injured healthy runs.

## 6. Failure Archaeology and the Adjudicated Diagnosis <!-- budget: 1.0pp -->

**Chain of death.** Each of the 27 injected treatment cells was classified along *compile → fire → escalate → judge → pause → attribute*. Of 18 misses: 1 had no covering tripwire (L1); **12 were compiled-but-never-fired (L2) — the dominant class**; 2 fired but were never escalated; 2 were escalated and ruled noise; 1 injection never fired <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §1 -->. The failure was not blindness at compile time — coverage misses were ≈ 0 once a heuristic artifact was corrected <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §A.6 --> — it was silence at observation time.

**Adjudication.** Rather than self-grading, we sent the diagnosis and redesign to two frontier-model red teams for blind review, then adjudicated every trace-testable [FATAL]/[MAJOR] claim empirically with a deterministic replay battery over the banked corpus — pre-commitments fixed before any battery result was readable; total LLM cost of the battery, $0 <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md header -->. The battery rests on byte-identical replay of all 27 injected worlds. Eighteen claims were dispositioned, roughly half holding per rater — justifying *adjudicating* external review rather than deferring to it or dismissing it <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §G -->. The decisive discriminations: the rival story that evidence reached the wire and died before the matcher is **refuted** (9/12 starved cells show zero raw post-injection observations — nothing existed to lose); the story that false interrupts replanned the surface out of the plan is **partial** (the strict signature appears in 0/12, but 7/12 were noise-consumed deaths); and base-agent horizon failure is **refuted** (died-before-oracle 0/17) <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §0 -->.

**The load-bearing result.** The original v1 diagnosis blamed the world's geometry — single-visit surfaces nobody could re-observe. The same-seed trajectory analysis corrected it and is the strongest evidence for the "not intelligence-bounded" half of the finding: **on identical seeds, the baseline systems re-observed every starved surface (12/12) while the sentinel did not** <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §B -->. The dumb baselines saw what the smart monitor missed. What starved the sentinel was its own noise economy — every false fire spends turns and lifetime that detection itself needs; one cell logged 172 post-injection fires on uninjected surfaces before dying at its escalation cap <!-- src: archaeology_v1.md §1 -->. One mechanism produced four gate symptoms: noise starved recall (KG1), the credulous judge generated the noise (KG2), and grinds and churn destroyed clean runs and waste (KG3, KG4's waste arm). The opening specimen (§1) is this coupling in a single cell.

**Errata, kept visible.** Two v1 diagnostic claims were corrected by the battery and remain in the record with their corrections: the "single-visit surface" starvation mechanism was withdrawn for its own example cell (the passage was fetched only *post*-injection — the original narrative was inverted by its own trace), and "exactly one matcher defect" became a *dead-pattern class* — 84 armed URL patterns that could never match any world path under the live dialect, repaired with a compile-time pattern-liveness regression (deviation D24) <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §A.2,§A.3; deviations.md D24 -->.

**Mechanism validated before construction.** Deterministic probes were mechanically constructed for all nine pilot injections and replayed against re-instantiated mutated worlds under the corroboration policy. Probe-primary corroboration blocked **18/18 false interrupts** while projected strict recall *rose* to 21/27, because probes recover what interrupts lose <!-- src: archaeology_v2.md §E.4 -->. **Every number here is a projected ceiling under stated assumptions** — mechanically constructed probes the LLM compiler had not yet produced, fixed trajectories, the same nine leaked injections — an upper-bound feasibility test of the mechanism, quoted nowhere in this paper as a result <!-- src: v6_1 §11.5 projection optimism -->. The replay also broke one drafted v2 clause (second-signal corroboration, deleted) and measured the perturbation and overhead constraints of §3.

## 7. Confirmatory Study <!-- budget: 1.25pp -->

The confirmatory study is the legitimate rerun path articulated *before* the pilot verdict existed: report v1 as it stands, diagnose from traces, change the design with cited evidence, re-measure under fresh pre-registration <!-- src: v6_1 §11.10 -->. Its integrity rules are in ink. All shared thresholds inherit v1's pre-verdict values verbatim (strict recall ≥ 60%; ≥ 50% in ≥ 4/5 categories; kill floor 40%; TTD ≥ 2×) — none derives from anything the diagnosis reported. New gates add probe validity as a hard gate, a recovery-quality clause (detect-only, detect-and-recover, and detect-and-justified-abort counted separately, so aborts cannot launder parity), and **absolute** noise caps replacing the unpassable ratio gate. Injection counters and payloads are re-drawn from fresh seeds the designers never see; the two held-out categories sit inside the recall denominator; a data embargo and dev-run ledger stand throughout; and the compiler is category-blind, so the held-out categories are unseen in the very vocabulary it reasons with, not only in their escrowed parameters <!-- src: decision_memo_phase1.md §4,§5; deviations.md D25/D27 -->.

**Result.** The matrix ran to completion — **172/172 cells banked**, 43 cells/arm × 4 arms (S1, S2, S3, v2), over 21 original-injected + 10 held-out + 12 clean per arm — and the gates computed once <!-- src: gate_report_final.json; results.jsonl -->.

```
1bKG1 detection: recall 10/15 = 66.7% (>=60%, kill floor clear);
     5/5 seen categories >=50% (API 6/6, SCHEMA_DRIFT 3/3, AUTH 6/6,
     TOOL_CONTRACT 3/3, RETRIEVAL_INTEGRITY 3/3); probe-validity 10/10;
     recovery-quality 17/24 = 70.8%             -> detection sub-terms PASS
1bKG2 noise/self-harm: clean median FIR 0, P95 0, max 0; 0 grinds;
     pre-detection median FIR 0; clean success 8/12 = 66.7%        -> PASS
1bKG3 economics: V2 clean median $0.3642 vs batch $0.2342;
     clean overhead 55.5% (cap <=12%)                             -> FAIL
1bKG4 vs heartbeat: V2 wasted 7008 vs S3 6404 = 1.09x;
     waste-parity not met (S3 detects 0/31)                       -> FAIL
OVERALL                                                           -> FAIL
```

The verdict is **FAIL, driven by 1bKG3 economics** — strong detection that fails the efficiency bar. One reporting nuance is kept visible: an admissibility precondition (an instrumentation-integrity replay) returned 123/124 byte-identical, the single non-identity being a benign net-neutral ordering artifact on the non-detecting heartbeat arm, characterized and logged as deviation D34; the gate code *folds* this precondition into the 1bKG1 boolean, so the composite prints `1bKG1=FAIL` even though all four detection sub-terms PASS. We report it decomposed — detection sub-terms PASS, precondition OK with one benign non-identity — and note that the OVERALL FAIL is unaffected, resting on 1bKG3 <!-- src: gate_report_final.json; deviations.md D34 -->.

**Detection inventory.** v2 detected **24/31** injected cells; the passive baselines were inert (batch S1 **0/31**, heartbeat S3 **0/31**) and naive S2 detected **12/31** <!-- src: results.jsonl -->. On the five seen categories v2 was perfect, recovering exactly the content-shaped, single-visit categories (RETRIEVAL_INTEGRITY, TOOL_CONTRACT) that v1's passive predicates could not see (§5). The 24/31 detected (77%) and the 10/15 = 66.7% gate recall differ by *definition*, not discrepancy: detection counts every injected cell, while the gate denominator is the recoverable subset that admits an oracle recovery path; the 24 detections split into detect-and-recover 3 + detect-and-justified-abort 14 + detect-only 7, with 17/24 = 70.8% in the two passing buckets <!-- src: gate_report_final.json 1bKG1 -->. Per-category recall carries Wilson 95% lower bounds; three categories sit at n=3, where a single cell swings 33 points (reported with the bound) <!-- src: gate_report_final.json categorical -->.

**Generalization to held-out.** v2 detected the held-out RESOURCE_BUDGET **3/5** where every baseline scored **0/5** — genuine transfer of the category-blind change-shape compiler to a structurally-unseen category <!-- src: results.jsonl -->. The second held-out category, DEPENDENCY_VERSION (silent minor-version pagination truncation), was missed by every arm including v2 (**0/5**). Read-only trace verification over all 20 DV cells locates the mechanism: the injection fires at the first tool call, *before* v2's arm-time baseline sweep, so v2's reference frame is itself already truncated and the post-injection re-reads match it; and no surface carries a version field, so the passive and naive baselines have no error or status signal to flag either <!-- src: results.jsonl; runs/matrix_1b/dv_claim_verification.md -->. Because the miss is harness-wide — including the anomaly-escalating naive baseline — it reads as a detection/benchmark limit, the read-side instance of the §6 observation-bound (a fault that fires before the monitor establishes its reference frame is uncatchable by baseline-diff), not a v2-specific generalization failure.

**v1's pathology, reversed.** v1's signature — a judge-rubber-stamped false-interrupt economy that starved recall and injured clean runs (median FIR 1.0) — is eliminated: clean-cell median FIR 0, P95 0, maximum 0 false interrupts, zero escalation-cap grinds <!-- src: gate_report_final.json 1bKG2 -->. The FIR-0 win is robust under real pressure: the clean set includes the legitimate `settings.yaml`-migration write that previously false-positived as drift, and v2 ran it at zero interrupts, while the naive baseline S2 false-fired on four clean cells <!-- src: docs/v3_archaeology.md G2 -->. v2 bought back recall *and* dropped noise to the naive baseline's zero floor; against S2's 12/31, sophistication helped here — but the help did not survive the economics gate, which is the subject of §8.

## 8. Coupling: A No-Free-Lunch Result <!-- budget: 1.0pp -->

The economic failures of v1 and v2 are not two missed targets. They are one converging fact, visible from four angles: detection and the cost of restoring observation are coupled, because the mechanism that restores observation is also the irreducible cost center.

**The cost is the capability.** A per-event cost autopsy of the v2 matrix decomposes the 55.5% clean overhead into a single net-new component: the once-per-run LLM compile-to-arm call (median $0.1376 on clean runs, against a V2−batch clean delta of $0.130), present in 12/12 clean cells. The entire detection substrate — probes, arming, corroboration, the event-gated cadence, the arm-time sweep — carries no measured cost <!-- src: docs/v3_archaeology.md G3; cost_autopsy_v3.json -->. The overhead is thus localized to the one step that cannot be removed without removing the capability: the compile is the LLM reasoning that produces the detection coverage *and* the category-blind generalization (§7). Cheapening, caching, or conditioning it is therefore not a free optimization — it puts at risk the very detection that is the paper's positive result. That mutual dependence is the coupling. A no-compile variant makes it concrete from the other side: removing the compile makes v2 nearly cost-neutral against batch on clean runs, so the compile *is* the cost center — although that variant's detection-equivalence is established only on its single-surface home case, so we report it as a cost-isolation, not a free lunch <!-- src: runs/matrix_1c/cv_pilot/CV_PILOT_SUMMARY.md; cv_result.json -->.

**The obvious rescue does not open.** A fixed per-run cost should amortize across fan-out, so the natural move is to spread the once-per-run compile over more parallel workers. The break-even model predicts a positive waste differential and an extrapolated crossover fan-out for v2 (in contrast to v1, which had a *negative* differential and no crossover at any fan-out) — but this is a model extrapolation from v2 data, a prediction to test, never a measured result (Appendix B) <!-- src: fanout_model_inputs.json; archaeology_v2.md §F -->. We built a width-scaled benchmark and ran a fan-out sizing pilot to test it. The pilot's point estimate — non-determinative by design, excluded from the sizing arithmetic — is that v2 is **costlier than batch at every tested width and fault rate, with the gap widening as width grows**, because the LLM compile (and its recompile-on-replan) scales with the planning the architecture does, not with the parallel work it could dilute against <!-- src: runs/matrix_1c/cv_pilot/CV_PILOT_SUMMARY.md; cv_result.json -->. We treat this as a **closing negative**, not a confirmatory result: the rescue path shows no sign of opening, and pinning it down at the pre-registered precision would cost roughly ten times the study budget (≈ $4,430 against a $450 budget, from the reliable narrow-width anchor) for a quantity already pointing the wrong way — so a confirmatory fan-out study is not warranted <!-- src: runs/matrix_1c/cv_pilot/cv_result.json (H=0.015; n≈128 at N=8); CV_PILOT_SUMMARY.md -->. The affordability gap is the incidental note; the direction is the finding.

**The floor still fails.** The benchmark is a deterministic mock in which re-observation is *free* — probes and sweeps re-fetch endpoints and re-read files at zero measured cost. This is the most generous possible cost environment for the architecture: it charges nothing for the very observation the architecture exists to restore. And it *still* fails the budget — on the one-time compile alone, for a structural reason, not on runtime monitoring. The measured 55.5% overhead is therefore a **lower bound**: in a live deployment, probe volume and sweep frequency that read as free here re-emerge as real cost and latency, with the compiler's family-arming over-provisioning (a median run arms many more probes than it exercises) as the canary <!-- src: docs/v3_archaeology.md mock-floor caveat; G3 -->. An architecture that cannot pay for itself in the environment most favorable to it does not pay for itself.

The synthesis is one sentence. Restoring observation fixes detection (§7) and eliminates the false-interrupt self-harm (§7), but the only localized cost lever is the capability-critical compile, the obvious amortization does not open, and the result holds even with re-observation priced at zero — so in budget-bounded multi-agent execution, **detection and economy are coupled, and compiled monitoring does not yet pay for itself.** This is the v3 problem, and it is a new pre-registration cycle, not a patch.

## 9. Threats to Validity <!-- budget: 0.5pp -->

**Scope of the claim.** These are findings of one setting, not laws: one harness, one model family, one mocked world, n=3 seeds with gates written on medians. Plan-shape variance (orchestrator consolidation) is real and uncontrolled by construction. Injected invalidations approximate but do not sample the natural distribution; manipulation qualification guarantees each wounds the baseline, not that it is representative. We state the boundedness results as empirical regularities with antecedents in partial-observability and monitorability theory, not as proofs.

**The mock floor is a strength here, and a bound elsewhere.** The free-re-observation mock makes the economic verdict *conservative* — the architecture fails the budget in the environment most favorable to it (§8) — but it cannot settle deployment-time viability, where probe cost and latency re-emerge. The 55.5% overhead is a lower bound, and the localized compile cost is also the capability, so the economic fix is not free of detection risk; both are named, neither is solved.

**Benchmark leak into the redesign.** v2 was designed from the pilot's traces on these nine injections. Mitigations: fresh parameter draws the designers never see, the escrowed held-out categories inside the recall denominator (drawn by an unseeded cryptographic procedure with a committed hash), and a category-blind compiler whose blindness is verifiable by inspection of the frozen prompt. The leak is acknowledged, not laundered <!-- src: v6_1 §11.5; deviations.md D33 -->. The held-out categories are held out in parameters and presence, not in *kind* — same hands, same ontology — and the batch baseline defeated two of three designs before one qualified, evidence that authoring genuinely wounding faults is itself hard; a held-out-*ontology* arm and a natural-failure arm remain the principal generalization tests for future work.

**Builder bias.** Benchmark, treatment, and gates share authorship. The counterweights are the apparatus: frozen pre-registration, pre-committed branches, custody pins, binding external red-team adjudication with trace-level dispositions, a public escrow hash, and negative results at full prominence — including a naive baseline beating the proposed architecture (§5). The strongest evidence the apparatus binds is behavioral: it returned a kill verdict on its authors' own architecture, twice, and the branches executed as written.

<!-- ============================================================ -->
## 10. Conclusion <!-- budget: 0.5pp -->

We pre-registered an architecture we believed in, and the gates we froze killed most of it; we diagnosed why from a byte-replayable corpus, rebuilt the detector around the diagnosis, and the gates killed the rebuild too — on cost. Because both verdicts came with their mechanism rather than a shrug, they compose into a single finding. In budget-bounded multi-agent LLM execution, failure detection is observation-bounded, not intelligence-bounded: a correct monitor that re-observes detects, and the dumb baselines that kept re-observing saw what the smart one starved itself out of seeing. And because the mechanism that restores observation — per-plan compilation — is the irreducible cost center, detection and economy are coupled: restoring observation fixes detection and ends the false-interrupt self-harm, but does not yet pay for itself, and the obvious amortization does not open even with re-observation priced at zero. That coupling, not any single missed bar, is the result, and it defines what a plan-compiled monitor must solve next.

## Appendix B — Fan-out break-even model (extrapolation, not a result) <!-- not counted toward 10pp body -->

The cost-positivity condition is `C + J + p·R < p·(W_batch − W_sent)` <!-- src: v6_1 §11.4 -->.
